## What you are buying

A payment of **£6** purchases the ability to create **one additional property survey** in the purchasing Urbanfold Survey account, once Stripe confirms the payment. It is a **single, non-recurring payment**, not a subscription. Existing surveys remain available to the account holder subject to the app's normal access rules.

This payment is for use of the survey application only. It does **not** include an architectural feasibility report, architectural or planning drawings, building regulations services, or advice from Urbanfold.

## Using Urbanfold for clients

The account holder may complete an additional property survey for their own property or on behalf of a client. A professional user must have appropriate authority to collect and upload survey information and photographs, explain how personal data will be processed, and only provide personal data for lawful purposes. Keep account credentials confidential and do not share your login.

Where a professional customer determines purposes and means of processing client information and Urbanfold processes that information on their behalf, the parties may need a separate Article 28 UK GDPR data-processing agreement before that use. This draft does not itself provide one.
